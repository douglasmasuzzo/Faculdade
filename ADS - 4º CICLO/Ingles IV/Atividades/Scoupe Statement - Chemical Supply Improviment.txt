## RESUMO - CONTEÚDO ##

Our project is called Chemical Laboratory Supply Improvement Project.

The project will improve the availability of chemical reagents by December next year.

Firstly, we will analyze the current inventory and identify the most used reagents.

Then, we will create a digital inventory control system to monitor quantities and expiration dates.

Then, we will organize a restocking schedule before practical classes.

Finally, a staff member will be responsible for monitoring and updating the inventory regularly.

The result is better practical classes, improved student learning, and a more organized laboratory.

This project helps students and teachers have more efficient practical lessons.
Thank you.
